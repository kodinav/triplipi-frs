Spiti Valley — premium circuit.

Seven days through Kaza, Key Monastery, Chandratal lakes, Langza fossils and the famous mid-night skies of the Himalayan high desert. Curated by Trans-Himalayan Expeditions, with full safety briefings and certified guides.

About this trip.

The Spiti Valley sits in the rain shadow of the Himalayas, a high cold desert where Buddhist culture has remained largely undisturbed for a thousand years. This seven-day premium circuit is designed for first-time visitors who want depth without compromise — every transfer in private SUVs, every night in carefully chosen homestays or boutique hotels, every guide certified in high-altitude protocols.

You'll travel from Shimla through the Kinnaur valley, acclimatising slowly as the altitude rises. Three days in and around Kaza let you visit Key Monastery at dawn, the highest village (Komic) by mid-morning, the fossil-rich Langza by afternoon. A night at Chandratal — when conditions allow — is the unofficial highlight: a glacial lake at 4,300m, dark enough that the Milky Way reads like a map.

Day-by-day itinerary.

A seven-day journey, paced for acclimatisation and considered moments.

  1. Day 1 · Shimla arrival Meet the group, walk the Mall Road, acclimatise gently.
  2. Day 2 · Shimla to Sangla Drive through Kinnaur, overnight in Sangla valley.
  3. Day 3 · Sangla to Kaza The drive into Spiti itself, with monastery stops along the way.
  4. Day 4 · Kaza day trips Key Monastery at dawn, Komic, Hikkim post office, Langza.
  5. Day 5 · Kaza to Chandratal Cross Kunzum La, camp at the moon lake.
  6. Day 6 · Chandratal to Manali Descend via Rohtang Pass.
  7. Day 7 · Manali departure Group breakfast, transfers to onward flights or trains.
