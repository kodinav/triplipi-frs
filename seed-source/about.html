The story

Triplipi began with a simple frustration.

For years, every trip we tried to plan began the same way — searching the same five sites, reading the same recycled paragraphs, clicking through the same affiliate funnels disguised as journalism. We wanted something different. A travel platform that read like a curated guidebook, looked like a magazine, and behaved like a friend with local knowledge.

So we built it. Triplipi launched in 2024 as a small editorial project — three writers, two photographers, a list of 60 destinations we'd actually been to. Two years later, we've indexed 450 places, partnered with 180 verified providers, and published more than 320 long-form stories. Everything else has changed except the original idea: depth over speed, and honesty about how we earn our money.

"The internet has more travel content than ever, and less useful travel writing than ever. We're trying to be a small correction to that."

— Editor, in conversation with Conde Nast Traveller India, March 2026

Mission & Vision

To make travel discovery feel less like research, and more like reading.

We believe that the best trips begin with the best stories. Our mission is to be the first place a curious traveller turns — not for booking, not for prices, but for the kind of honest, considered, photographically-rich account of a destination that helps you know whether you actually want to go.

When you're ready to book, we connect you to verified partners — with full disclosure of every commercial relationship, every time. Our editorial team has no commercial pressure to favour any partner over another, and we publish our methodology openly. Slow travel deserves slow journalism, and that's what we try to make.
