Overview

Triplipi ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have about how we handle your information. By using Triplipi.example, you agree to the practices described below.

Information we collect

1.1 — Information you provide

When you contact us, request a quote, or sign up for our newsletter, we collect the information you submit voluntarily, including your name, email address, phone number, and any message content. This information is used only to respond to you and is never sold to third parties.

1.2 — Automatically collected information

We collect standard server logs, including IP address, browser type, referring page, and time of visit. We use Google Analytics to understand aggregate traffic patterns. You can opt out of Google Analytics via your browser settings or by installing the official Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

1.3 — Cookies

We use cookies for session management, preferences, and analytics. See our Cookies Policy for the complete list and how to manage them.

How we use information

  • To respond to your inquiries and provide the services you request
  • To send newsletters or updates, if you have opted in
  • To improve our website, content, and user experience
  • To comply with legal obligations

External providers and affiliate links

Our platform includes links to verified partner websites, including travel package providers, hotels, and affiliate platforms. When you click an external link, a consent modal will appear informing you of:

  • The name and URL of the destination site
  • Our affiliate disclosure, if applicable
  • What browser information may be shared via standard HTTP referrer mechanisms

You must explicitly consent before any redirect occurs. No personal account information from Triplipi is shared with external providers.

Your rights

Under applicable data protection laws, you have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal information. To exercise any of these rights, please contact privacy@Triplipi.example. We will respond within 30 days.

Data retention

We retain personal information only as long as necessary for the purposes for which it was collected. Contact form submissions are retained for two years for record-keeping. Newsletter subscriptions are retained until you unsubscribe.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by posting a notice on our home page and updating the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the site after such notice constitutes acceptance of the changes.

Contact

For privacy-related inquiries, please write to privacy@Triplipi.example or use our contact form.
